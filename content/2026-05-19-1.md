TaskOrbit project needs realtime notification. I am using websocket to implement it. I have considered other options like SSE. I think websocket is the best one for it's low latency.

Websocket connection will be authorized when handshake happens. Client will send the access token as `Authorization` header. Backend will verify the token and establish the connection.
