I used to write blogs previously with a dream that I might get rich one day by becoming a great blogger writing about software. Whether it was a dream that could be achieved or not I don't know, because I stopped doing it.

This time I am writing blogs just for the purpose of documenting my thought and progress as a software engineer. It's fine if nobody reads it (though I won't lie I will really like if someone does read it).

Yesterday, I realized the benefits of writing down the things I am learning everyday by answering some subjective questins. I saw how it collects my thoughts and the possibility that in the future I can look back connect them together.

I also felt one doubt - what I know today may not be correct. Then?

Well it is embarrassing that few days back I completely misunderstood a concept, but that too shows some progress and tracks my thought process.

I am just rambling around with whatever comes to my mind right now. But one thing is that this will come handy in the future. It does not matter how I will feel embarrassingly bad I was looking back at it but I am hoping it will create a systematic thought process in me.

Btw, this post is just to test the setup with [quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz). I found this after some prompting on ChatGPT. Another option was [docusaurus](https://docusaurus.io/) but it felt too heavy for my current needs. It handles too many cases which I don't even know if I need or not. And I also read that quartz can be customized. Although I will think later when that requirement really comes. Right now quartz seems like the best choice for me.

I will deploy it on Github pages (because it is free). But it will be my first time. So, let's see how it goes...

Bye!
